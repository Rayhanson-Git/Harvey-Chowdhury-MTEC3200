# Class 2 — Interview Questions

## Research focus

Test the current problem statement against another person's actual experience with a workout-to-school or workout-to-work transition.

The interview should focus on specific events that already happened. Avoid hypothetical future questions, leading questions, and yes/no questions.

## Questions

### Context and the most recent routine

1. Think about the most recent day you worked out before school or work. Walk me through that day from the end of the workout to the start of your next commitment.
2. What time did your workout end that day, and what fixed commitment came next?
3. What did you have to do between finishing the workout and arriving at that commitment?
4. What, if anything, had you prepared beforehand for that specific day?

### A specific rushed or difficult transition

5. Tell me about the last time that transition felt rushed or stressful.
6. What happened immediately before you realized you were running behind?
7. How did you realize you were running behind?
8. What did you change, shorten, or skip once you realized you were behind?
9. Which step took longer than you expected that day, and what caused it?

### A smoother comparison and existing workarounds

10. Tell me about a recent day when the same transition went smoothly.
11. What was different on that day compared with the rushed example?
12. Tell me about the last time you used a routine, alarm, packed bag, hard stop, or another workaround to make the transition easier.
13. What happened after you used that workaround?

### Impact and meaning

14. Looking back at the examples you just described, which part of the transition was hardest to control, and why?
15. What did being rushed change about the rest of that day?

## Follow-up prompts

- Why?
- What happened next?
- Can you give me a specific example?
- What did you do at that point?
- What made that difficult?
- How did that affect the next part of your day?
