# Persona: the commuter exercising before a fixed commitment

Documented October 20, 2026. This is a behavioral composite derived from the recorded interviews, not an additional participant. No fictional age, income, photograph or quotation has been added.

## Situation and goal

A student or working person fits exercise before a scheduled class or shift. After the workout they still need to shower, change and travel. The goal is to leave enough time for that transition while protecting the next commitment.

Primary evidence: Chris's gym-to-class account and Selina's gym-to-work account. David supplies a contrasting case where a short, predictable walk and more buffer make the routine easier.

## Needs

- A clear time to stop exercise, with preparation still reserved before departure.
- A separate leave-by time based on the next commitment.
- An explicit allowance for uncertainty.
- A way to correct an estimate without re-entering the whole plan.

The first three needs follow from the interview patterns and existing workarounds. Easy editing is a design response to variable estimates and remains a usability hypothesis.

## Boundary of the persona

This persona emphasizes a constrained transition rather than every person who visits a gym. David's more predictable routine may create less need for a planner. Selina's mental pressure is documented in one account and is not assigned to every user. Technology preferences, willingness to adopt an app and long-term use remain untested.
