# Routine audit

Compiled October 20, 2026 from the existing [What / How / Why exercise](what_how_why.md) and [problem statement](problem_statement.md).

| Routine moment | Existing observation | Friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gym to class | Finish around 8:30 AM, shower/change and travel to a 10:00 AM class. | Required steps share a short window with limited buffer. |
| Class to work | Class ends at 3:00 or 5:00 PM depending on the day, followed by work. | Little time to rest, eat or reset. |
| Work to home | Travel home around 8:00 or 9:00 PM. | Fatigue accumulates across the day. |

The selected focus is the gym-to-class transition because it repeats, has a fixed deadline, and is narrow enough to investigate. The other transitions remain context.

These are previously recorded routine observations, not newly collected daily entries. The original September routine-audit document and a dated multi-day self-log are not included here. This compilation does not stand in for a new observation study.
