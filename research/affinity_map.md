# LeaveBy affinity map

Prepared October 20, 2026 from the three interview records dated September 19, 2026.

Status: assistant-assisted retrospective grouping; awaiting Harvey's review. This map documents an analysis made after the prototype was built. It does not imply that this grouping existed before development.

## Method and sources

Each card below contains one paraphrased observation from the recorded answers. Card IDs identify the participant and observation; the source column identifies the interview question so the observation can be checked. Cluster names and interpretations are analytical groupings, not participant quotations.

## Cluster 1 — Unexpected waits reduce available transition time

| Card | Observation | Source question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Waiting for a machine delayed gym departure; a subsequent train delay led to skipped breakfast and running to class. | [Chris](../interviews/Chris_2026-09-19.md#answers): last rushed transition |
| C2 | Train waiting can consume fifteen minutes, and the commute feels least controllable. | [Chris](../interviews/Chris_2026-09-19.md#answers): transition to class; greatest pressure |
| D1 | A crowded locker room can mean waiting for an available shower. | [David](../interviews/David_2026-09-19.md#answers): stressful transitions |
| S1 | Equipment waits in a crowded gym push the schedule back. | [Selina](../interviews/Selina_2026-09-19.md#answers): causes of delays |
| S2 | Showering and getting ready can take around ten minutes longer when busy. | [Selina](../interviews/Selina_2026-09-19.md#answers): time after the workout |

Interpretation to review: delays occur at different stages and can accumulate. Estimating only workout duration misses part of the transition.
