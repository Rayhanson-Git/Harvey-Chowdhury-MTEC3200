# Class 2 — Problem Statement

When I finish my morning workout before a fixed class, I have to shower, get ready, and commute to campus in a short window, which is frustrating because one small delay can make the entire transition feel rushed. I think this happens because I have very little buffer between the gym and class, while parts of the transition such as equipment availability, the locker room, and transit can be unpredictable. What I wish happened instead is having a more consistent buffer that can absorb small delays so I can arrive prepared instead of rushed.

## Check

- The statement focuses on one repeated moment rather than my entire schedule.
- It describes the friction before deciding what to build.
- It explains what happens and why it matters.
- It is narrow enough to test through interviews.
- It can change if the interviews show that my assumption is wrong.
